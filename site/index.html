    The process, end to end

    Ten stages. Only the first four interpret anything; the rest are plain rules on data AP already owns.
    Read
    01Receive file

    PDF or image. SHA-256 fingerprint so a re-sent file is recognised.

    02Read the document

    PDF text layer, or OCR when it's a scan. Low OCR confidence is flagged.

    03Extract fields

    Number, date, PO, totals, tax, terms, bank, emails, line items, each with the line it came from.

    04AI second reader

    Optional. Reads separately; disagreements on key fields go to a person.

    05Check the invoice

    Required fields, lines add up, total = subtotal + tax, date sanity, currency.

    Check against company records
    06Vendor and bank

    Vendor master by name and email domain. Bank account vs file, change wording, lookalike domains.

    07Duplicates

    Normalised invoice number (INV-0871 = CFL 871), amount and date window.

    08PO and receipt match

    Quoted or inferred PO. Per-line price tolerance, cumulative quantity, goods received.

    09Decide

    Fixed rule order; first match wins. Approve, hold or reject with owner and message.

    10Record and route

    ERP-ready record. Held invoices land in the Exceptions queue for a person.

    The edge cases, and why these four

    Decision rules, in order

    The first rule that matches sets the outcome. Every other failed check is still listed.

    Design choices

    Rules decide, AI reads. An AI model, chosen by the user (Gemini, ChatGPT, Grok or Claude), can help read messy documents, but the approve / hold / reject call is a fixed rule order on the company's own data. The same invoice always gets the same answer, and every answer can be explained line by line.

    Fraud and duplicates are checked before matching. A perfect three-way match says nothing about where the money goes. The Bluebird sample matches its PO exactly, and that is what makes its bank-change request convincing. The FBI's 2024 Internet Crime Report counted 21,442 business email compromise complaints and $2.77 billion in reported losses.

    Hold, don't guess. When the process isn't sure, such as an inferred PO or a scan it can't read, it holds the invoice and says exactly who has to do what. Partial problems still release the amount that can be paid.

    Tolerances are per line, the way Oracle Payables and SAP set them. Billing under the PO price passes; quantity is checked against what was ordered and, for goods, what was received, counting everything billed on that PO before.

    Assumptions. Invented company and data; USD payments; one invoice per file, first three pages read; vendors, POs and receipts would come from the ERP in production; drafted messages are never sent automatically.

    Document

    AI second reader

    An AI model reads each invoice separately from the rules. If the two readings disagree on a key field, a person checks it. The AI never approves, holds or rejects anything.
